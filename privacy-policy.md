# WB Squad Uploader - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

WB Squad Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to post their own videos to their own
accounts. It has no other users.

**What it stores**
- The login tokens the owner grants when connecting their own TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram
  accounts. These are used only to upload the owner's videos.
- Videos waiting to be posted, until they are posted.

**Where it's stored**
- On the owner's own computer, and in the owner's private GitHub repository (tokens in GitHub's
  encrypted Secrets). Videos are deleted from GitHub once posted.

**What it doesn't do**
- It does not collect data about other people, viewers or followers.
- It does not sell, share or send any data to anyone other than the platforms the owner posts to.
- It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.

**From TikTok specifically**, it uses only the `user.info.basic` and `video.publish` permissions, to
check the account and publish the owner's videos.

**Removing access.** The owner can disconnect at any time from their TikTok settings
(Security and permissions -> Manage app permissions) and by deleting the tokens from the tool.

Contact: through the WB Squad TikTok account that owns this app.
